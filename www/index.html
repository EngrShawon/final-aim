HOW TO PLAY

  • Mouse aim · Right click / Z scope on/off · Left click shoot · Wheel zoom (scoped)
  • Shots without the scope are inaccurate — scope in for precision.
  • R reload · Shift hold breath (steadies aim) · Esc pause
  • Touch: drag to aim, use on-screen buttons.
  • You are a professional sniper in a fictional training/mission scenario. Complete each target challenge without graphic effects.
  • Head hits score most. Far shots and quick follow-ups earn bonuses. Reach the score goal before ammo or time runs out. Cover blocks bullets. Every 10th mission is a timed Elite mission.
